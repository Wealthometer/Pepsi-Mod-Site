OUR HISTORY

Discover the story of Pepsi, from its humble beginnings to becoming a global icon

The Pepsi Journey

1893

The Beginning

Caleb Bradham, a pharmacist in New Bern, North Carolina, creates "Brad's Drink," a refreshing beverage that would later be known as Pepsi-Cola.

1898

Pepsi-Cola is Born

Brad's Drink is renamed "Pepsi-Cola," a name that reflects the two main ingredients: pepsin (a digestive enzyme) and kola nuts.

1902

Business Growth

The Pepsi-Cola Company is officially incorporated, with Caleb Bradham as the president. The business begins to expand rapidly.

1940s

Breaking Barriers

Pepsi becomes one of the first major companies to feature African Americans in its advertisements, breaking racial barriers in marketing.

1950s

The Pepsi Generation

Pepsi launches its groundbreaking "The Pepsi Generation" campaign, targeting a younger demographic and establishing itself as the choice of a new generation.

1964

Diet Pepsi Launch

Pepsi introduces Diet Pepsi, becoming one of the first major beverage companies to offer a diet cola option.

1984

Michael Jackson Partnership

Pepsi signs Michael Jackson for a record-breaking $5 million sponsorship deal, revolutionizing celebrity endorsements in advertising.

1990s-2000s

Global Expansion

Pepsi continues to expand globally, introducing new products and acquiring other food and beverage brands to become PepsiCo, a global powerhouse.

Today

Innovation Continues

Pepsi continues to innovate with new flavors, zero-sugar options, and sustainable packaging, while maintaining its position as one of the world's most recognized brands.

Our Legacy

For over a century, Pepsi has been more than just a refreshing beverage. It's been a cultural icon, a pioneer in advertising, and a brand that continues to push boundaries.

From our humble beginnings in a North Carolina pharmacy to becoming one of the world's most recognized brands, our journey has been defined by innovation, bold choices, and a commitment to bringing joy to our consumers.
