Frequently Asked Questions

How can I find Pepsi products near me?

You can use our Product Locator tool on our website to find Pepsi products at stores near you. Simply enter your zip code or city to see a list of retailers in your area.

How can I provide feedback about a Pepsi product?

We value your feedback! You can share your thoughts about our products by filling out the contact form on this page, calling our customer service line, or reaching out to us on social media.

How can I request Pepsi sponsorship for an event?

For sponsorship inquiries, please send an email to sponsorships@pepsi.com with details about your event, including date, location, expected attendance, and sponsorship opportunities.

How can I apply for a job at PepsiCo?

Visit our Careers page at pepsicojobs.com to explore current job openings and submit your application. We offer opportunities in various fields, including marketing, sales, research, manufacturing, and more.
